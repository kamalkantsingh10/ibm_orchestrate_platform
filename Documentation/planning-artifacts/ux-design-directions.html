Direction 1 — Editorial

Magazine meets workspace · Stripe-docs-tilted
Typography and whitespace carry almost everything. Panels breathe at 20–24 px padding. Case titles sit at 28 px. The cockpit feels like reading a well-set documentation site. Least dense direction — confident, readable, beautiful, but requires trust that the officer accepts slower visual scan.

Direction 2 — Terminal

Bloomberg-tilted, speed-first, dense
Maximum data-per-pixel within the marble palette. Queue rows tighten to ~34 px. Panel titles shrink to 10 px uppercase mono. Three-column canvas grid. Monospace bleeds into labels. Feel: a pilot's cockpit at cruising altitude — rigorous, professional, fast. Best for fluent senior officers; risks intimidating new users.

Direction 3 — Calm Canvas ★ Chosen · Finexy-refined

Calm aesthetic · Terminal-tight density · unified decomposition across all four panels
Calm Canvas tuned to Terminal-level density: 12 px body, 28 px panel summary numbers (bolder, sculptural), 14 px panel padding, 10 px corner radius, 14 px panel gap. Every panel now follows one visual grammar — a summary line at the top (big numeric + label + band), a hairline divider, then labeled decomposition rows with proportional bars and monospace values aligned right. Entity Verification shows source status. Risk Score shows component contributions. UBO shows ownership percentages with directors above shareholders. Screening shows per-dimension match confidence. The Screening panel, carrying the one amber hit, fills softly with amber tint — a single hero panel, unmissable without shouting. Decision Zone rationale sets in Source Serif. Primary action is a pill-shaped black button. Finexy-inspired refinements where they serve the marble aesthetic; nothing that pushes us toward consumer-app.

Direction 4 — Atelier

Aesthetic-forward · marble texture realized · spring flowers worn lightly
Pushes the marble metaphor further with a whisper of texture (very faint CSS gradients on the canvas — still flat, still professional). Agent faces are bigger and more present. Each panel carries a subtle 2 px accent line in the colour of its responsible agent — a "spring flower" leak that's visible but light. Decision Zone rationale is set in Source Serif, invoking a document feel. Boldest aesthetic of the four; highest risk of feeling "designed" rather than "workspace."
